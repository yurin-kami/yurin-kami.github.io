# PRODUCT

## Register
brand

## Users
KAMISHOW is a multilingual personal blog and knowledge site for readers who move between essays, technical notes, tags, and search.

## Purpose
The product should feel calm, expressive, and easy to read for long sessions while keeping the author's pink-forward identity visible across light and dark modes.

## Brand Personality
Soft, lively, studious, and slightly playful. The UI should feel like a carefully kept reading room rather than a generic monochrome blog template.

## Anti-References
Avoid harsh pure-black dark modes, generic gray-only palettes, and dark themes that drop the site's pink brand color.

## Design Principles
- Preserve readability first, especially in article bodies, search, tags, and legacy pages.
- Keep the brand color active in dark mode through accents, glows, borders, and interactive states.
- Theme changes should feel immediate and intentional, with no flash of the wrong theme.
- Automatic theme mode follows the browser's local clock: light from 07:00 to 18:59, dark from 19:00 to 06:59.

## Accessibility
Maintain visible focus, adequate contrast, and respect reduced-motion preferences.
